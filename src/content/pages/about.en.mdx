## What is 73-9?

**73-9** is both a historic NBA regular-season record and the name of a free browser challenge at [73-9.org](https://73-9.org). You start with one hundred million dollars, spin a wheel five times, and try to build a starting five that can stand up to the 2015-16 Golden State Warriors—the club that finished 73-9.

## Where the name comes from

In 2015-16 the Warriors won seventy-three games and lost nine, edging past the 1995-96 Chicago Bulls' 72-10 for the best regular-season mark the league has recorded. Fans call that milestone **73-9**. This game turns the benchmark into a playable puzzle: a wheel lands on a random team-season somewhere between 1990 and today; you may sign exactly one player from that roster; everything you spend comes out of a single bankroll.

## How it works

1. Spin the wheel to a random team-season.
2. Sign one player for the current position while staying under the $100M cap.
3. Repeat five times (point guard through center).
4. Run an eighty-two-game simulation and see how often your five beats the Warriors who authored 73-9.

Player ratings draw on historical box scores and minutes; prices map production to a modern salary-cap scale. Results are entertainment, not predictions or betting advice.

## Independent fan project

73-9.org is an independent fan project. It is **not** affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA, any team, or any player. Player names and statistics appear for information and game simulation only.

## Play free

The experience is free, runs in your browser, and needs no account or download. Open [73-9.org](https://73-9.org), spin the wheel, and spend the hundred million wisely.
