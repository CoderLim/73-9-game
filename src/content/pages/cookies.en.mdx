## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how 73-9.org ("73-9", "we", "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit the site, play the game, sign in, read an article, or use another feature. It should be read together with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

Cookies are not the only technology covered here. Browsers can also store information through local storage, session storage, IndexedDB, pixels, tags, and similar tools. In this policy, “cookies” may be used as a convenient general term for those technologies when the same explanation applies.

The exact technologies used can change as the site is improved. We update this page when a material practice changes.

## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file placed on your browser or device by a website. It can contain an identifier or a small amount of information that allows a site to remember a session, preference, or previous interaction.

A first-party cookie is associated with the site you are visiting. A third-party cookie is associated with another provider whose service is used on the page, such as an analytics or advertising provider.

Similar storage technologies can perform related functions without using a traditional cookie. For example, local storage can remember a language setting or an unfinished game state on the same device.

## Essential site functions

Some cookies or storage are used because the site cannot provide a requested function reliably without them. Examples may include:

- Maintaining a secure signed-in session.
- Protecting forms and APIs from abuse.
- Remembering the selected language.
- Keeping a temporary game state while you move through a run.
- Caching game data on the device to reduce repeated downloads.
- Preserving a consent choice so the same message is not shown on every page.

Blocking these technologies can cause sign-in, language, saved progress, or other requested features to stop working.

## Local game data

73-9 can store game-related information locally in your browser. Depending on the current build, this may include downloaded data caches, a draft in progress, device-level achievements, a previous result, or interface preferences.

Local data normally stays on the device until it expires, is replaced, or you clear site data. A locally stored item is not automatically linked to an account. When you choose an account feature such as saving a leaderboard result, the information needed for that feature may be sent to our server as described in the Privacy Policy.

Clearing browser data may remove local progress and require the game dataset to be downloaded again.

## Analytics technologies

We may use Google Analytics and/or a privacy-focused analytics provider to understand how the site performs. Analytics can help answer questions such as:

- Which pages are visited most often.
- Whether visitors encounter errors.
- Which device or browser categories need improvement.
- How long pages take to load.
- Whether a navigation or game step is confusing.

Analytics data is used for aggregate product improvement, security, and performance monitoring. We do not intentionally place an email address, phone number, or other directly identifying information into analytics page URLs, event names, or custom parameters.

Where consent is required, optional analytics storage should not be activated until an appropriate choice has been recorded. Browser controls, extensions, and provider-level opt-out tools may offer additional choices.

## Google advertising cookies

When Google AdSense advertising is enabled, Google and other authorized advertising vendors may use cookies or similar identifiers to serve and measure ads.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to 73-9.org or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). Additional industry opt-out choices may be available through [YourAdChoices](https://youradchoices.com/) or similar services, depending on region.

Advertising may be non-personalized, limited, or unavailable when consent has not been provided, when regional rules require a different mode, or while the site is under advertising review.

## Consent for the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

When Google ads are served to visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Google requires publishers using personalized advertising to use a Google-certified consent management platform that integrates with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

73-9.org may use Google’s Privacy & messaging tools or another Google-certified CMP to display the applicable consent message. The message may let eligible users accept, reject, or manage purposes and vendors. A link or control for reviewing privacy choices may be provided where the CMP supports it.

The presence of this policy does not replace a required consent interface. Advertising should remain disabled until the required account-side CMP configuration is published and verified.

## How to manage your choices

You can control cookies and similar technologies in several ways:

1. **Consent message:** Use the choices shown in the site’s privacy or consent message when available.
2. **Browser settings:** Block, delete, or limit cookies for all sites or specifically for 73-9.org.
3. **Clear site data:** Remove cookies, local storage, IndexedDB, and cached files for this domain.
4. **Private browsing:** Use a private or incognito session, understanding that some data may still exist during that session.
5. **Google advertising controls:** Review personalized-ad settings through Google Ads Settings.
6. **Extensions or device controls:** Use browser privacy tools, content blockers, or platform-level tracking controls.

Blocking all storage can reduce functionality. For example, you may be signed out, lose a local draft, reset a language choice, or need to download game data again.

## Retention

Different technologies have different lifetimes. A session cookie may disappear when the browser closes. A preference may remain longer so the site can remember a choice. Local game caches may remain until replaced or cleared. Third-party providers set retention periods under their own policies and account settings.

We aim to keep first-party information only for as long as it is useful for the stated purpose, security, or a legal requirement.

## Third-party providers

The providers present on a page depend on the features currently enabled. They may include hosting, authentication, analytics, error monitoring, and advertising services.

Third parties process information under their own privacy and cookie policies. Enabling a service does not give that provider permission to use information for unrelated purposes beyond its terms, settings, and applicable law.

We periodically review integrations and remove tools that are no longer needed.

## Do Not Track and browser signals

Browsers and devices may send privacy signals such as Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track. Legal requirements and provider support for these signals vary. We evaluate recognized signals where they apply and configure advertising or data processing controls accordingly.

You can also contact us when you believe a regional privacy right applies to your information.
