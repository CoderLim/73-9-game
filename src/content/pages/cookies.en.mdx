## Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how 73-9.org ("we") uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage when you visit or play. It should be read with our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy).

## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Similar technologies (local storage, session storage, pixels) can store or read identifiers for the same kinds of purposes.

## How We Use Them

| Category        | Purpose                                                                          | Examples                                                                |
| --------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Essential**   | Make the site work (security, load balancing, locale preference, basic session). | Locale cookie, session or auth cookies if you sign in                   |
| **Functional**  | Remember preferences that improve play or browsing.                              | UI preferences stored locally                                           |
| **Analytics**   | Understand aggregate traffic and improve the experience.                         | Google Analytics; privacy-focused analytics (e.g. Plausible) if enabled |
| **Advertising** | Show and measure ads, if advertising is enabled.                                 | Third-party ad cookies (only if we turn ads on)                         |

Essential cookies are generally required for the Service to function. Analytics and advertising cookies (when used) are optional in the sense that you can block them via browser controls, though some measurement may then be incomplete.

## Third-Party Cookies

If we embed or enable third-party tools (analytics, ads, fonts, or video), those parties may set their own cookies under their policies. We only enable providers we believe are needed to run or improve the site.

## Managing Cookies

You can:

- Delete or block cookies in your browser settings.
- Use private/incognito mode.
- Clear site data for 73-9.org (this may reset language or local game state).

Blocking essential cookies may break sign-in or language selection. Blocking analytics usually does not stop you from playing.

## Local Game Data

Gameplay may store temporary state on your device (for example progress within a session). That data typically stays local unless a feature you use needs to send it to our servers.

We may update this Cookie Policy when our practices change. Check the date at the top of this page.
