## Introduction

73-9.org ("73-9", "we", "us") is a free browser basketball simulation game. This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect when you visit or play, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site, you agree to this policy.

## Who We Are

73-9 is an independent fan project at [https://73-9.org](https://73-9.org). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA, any team, or any player. For privacy questions, email [support@73-9.org](mailto:support@73-9.org).

## Information We Collect

Core gameplay runs in your browser and does not require an account. Depending on how you use the site, we may process:

1. **Usage and device data** — pages visited, approximate region from IP, browser type, device type, and similar technical logs needed to operate and secure the site.
2. **Cookies and similar technologies** — small files or local storage used for essential site functions, preferences, analytics, and (if enabled) advertising. See our [Cookie Policy](/cookies).
3. **Optional account data** — if you choose to create an account on features that require sign-in, we may store email address, display name, and authentication-related data.
4. **Communications** — if you email us, we keep the content of that message and your contact details to respond.
5. **Game progress stored locally** — some preferences or session state may live only on your device (for example in local storage) and are not sent to us unless needed for a feature you use.

We do not ask for payment card numbers for the free game. We do not sell your personal information.

## How We Use Information

We use information to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve the game and website.
- Understand aggregate traffic and fix bugs or abuse.
- Remember language or other preferences where applicable.
- Respond to support requests.
- Comply with law and protect the security of the service.

## Analytics and Advertising

We may use analytics tools such as Google Analytics and privacy-conscious analytics (for example Plausible or similar) and, if advertising is enabled in the future, third-party ad networks. Those providers may set their own cookies or receive truncated technical data subject to their policies. You can limit non-essential cookies through your browser settings and our Cookie Policy guidance.

## Data Sharing

We share data only when needed:

- With infrastructure and service providers that help us host, secure, or measure the site, under appropriate agreements.
- When required by law or to respond to valid legal process.
- To protect our rights, users, or the public from harm or abuse.
- If the project is transferred, with notice where required.

We do not sell personal information.

## Data Retention

We keep technical logs and support emails only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or anonymize them. Locally stored game data remains on your device until you clear it.

## Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact [support@73-9.org](mailto:support@73-9.org). You may also clear cookies and site data in your browser at any time.

## Children's Privacy

The site is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your region). If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

## International Visitors

The site may be hosted or accessed from servers in different countries. By using 73-9.org you understand that information may be processed outside your home country with appropriate safeguards where required.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of the page will change when we do. Continued use after changes means you accept the updated policy.
