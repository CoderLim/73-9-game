## Introduction

73-9.org ("73-9", "we", "us") provides a free browser basketball strategy game and related editorial guides. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you visit, play, create an optional account, save a result, read an article, or contact us.

The core game can be played without creating an account. Some data is stored only on your device. Other information is processed by our servers or service providers when needed to deliver a feature, protect the site, measure performance, or display advertising where permitted.

This policy should be read with our [Cookie Policy](/cookies) and [Terms of Service](/terms-of-service).

## Who we are

73-9.org is an independent fan project available at [https://73-9.org](https://73-9.org). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the NBA, any team, or any player.

For privacy questions or requests, email [support@73-9.org](mailto:support@73-9.org).

## Information processed without an account

When you visit the site, systems used to deliver and secure the service may process technical information such as:

- IP address and approximate region derived from it.
- Browser, device, operating system, and language.
- Requested page, referring page, and timestamps.
- Network and performance information.
- Security, rate-limit, and error logs.
- Consent status and cookie preferences where applicable.
- Aggregate interaction information used to understand page or game performance.

We do not intentionally collect precise GPS location for the core game. We do not require payment-card information because the current core experience is free.

## Local game and preference data

The game may use browser storage such as local storage, session storage, or IndexedDB. This can hold downloaded game data, an unfinished draft, device-level achievements, language choices, cached information, or a previous result.

Local information normally remains on the device and is not automatically connected to an identity. You can remove it by clearing site data in your browser. Doing so may remove progress and require the browser to download game resources again.

When you choose a server feature, such as saving an eligible result, the specific information needed for that feature may be transmitted to us.

## Optional account information

If you create or use an account, we may process:

- Email address.
- Display name and profile image, when provided.
- Authentication provider identifiers or security tokens.
- Account creation and last-sign-in timestamps.
- Saved game results and leaderboard information.
- Records needed to prevent abuse or maintain account security.

Passwords may be handled by an authentication provider or stored only in a protected one-way form, depending on the sign-in method. We do not ask you to send passwords by email.

You may play as a guest when you do not want a saved account history.

## Communications

When you email us or submit a support request, we process the message, contact address, attachments, and any technical details you provide. We use this information to respond, investigate bugs, correct data, enforce the Terms, or maintain a record of the request.

Do not send government identifiers, financial details, health information, or other sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for a specific legal request.

## How we use information

We use information for the following purposes:

- Deliver the website, game, articles, and account features.
- Maintain sessions, preferences, and saved results.
- Secure the service and detect spam, fraud, automated abuse, or attacks.
- Diagnose errors and improve speed, compatibility, and usability.
- Understand aggregate traffic and feature usage.
- Respond to support, correction, and privacy requests.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.
- Display and measure advertising when advertising is enabled and permitted.
- Keep records needed to demonstrate consent or privacy choices.

We do not sell personal information for money. We do not use health, religion, sexual orientation, or other sensitive categories to create advertising audiences.

## Analytics

We may use Google Analytics and/or a privacy-focused analytics provider to measure aggregate traffic and product performance.

Analytics may process page views, device category, approximate location, referral information, and interaction events. We configure analytics with the goal of avoiding directly identifying information. Email addresses, phone numbers, and similar personal data should not be placed in page URLs, analytics event names, or custom dimensions.

Where law requires consent for analytics storage, the applicable consent controls should be used before optional analytics cookies are activated.

## Advertising and Google AdSense

73-9.org may use Google AdSense to support hosting and development. During an advertising review or when required consent controls are not ready, the ad-serving script may be disabled even though site ownership remains verifiable through AdSense account metadata and ads.txt.

When Google ads are enabled:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s previous visits to this website or other websites.
- Google’s use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- Users may manage or opt out of personalized advertising through [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- Other authorized vendors or ad networks may use cookies subject to their own policies and available opt-out tools.
- Ad requests may receive technical information such as IP address, device details, page context, and consent signals.

We do not intentionally pass an email address, phone number, full name, or other directly identifying account information in an AdSense URL, ad parameter, or audience label.

Advertising should not be shown on empty, loading, error, sign-in, account, or other low-value screens. It should remain secondary to the game and editorial content.

## Consent in the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland

Google requires publishers serving personalized ads in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland to use a Google-certified consent management platform integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework.

When advertising is enabled for visitors in those regions, 73-9.org may use Google Privacy & messaging or another Google-certified CMP to make disclosures, request choices, and send consent signals.

Ad serving should remain disabled until the CMP configuration has been published and tested.

Users may be offered options to accept, reject, or manage purposes and vendors. Available choices can differ by region and provider configuration.

## When information is shared

We may share limited information with:

- Hosting, database, storage, authentication, email, monitoring, analytics, and advertising providers that operate parts of the service.
- Professional advisers when needed for legal, security, or compliance matters.
- Authorities or other parties when required by valid legal process.
- Parties involved in investigating abuse, protecting users, or defending rights.
- A successor if the project or relevant assets are transferred, subject to applicable notice requirements.

Providers receive only the information reasonably needed for their role and process it under their terms, instructions, and applicable law.

We do not publish an account email address on the leaderboard. Public leaderboard fields should be limited to the display information and game result intended for that feature.

## Data retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the stated purpose, security, disputes, backups, or law. Local browser data remains until it expires, is replaced, or you clear it. Account and saved-result data may remain while an account is active. Logs and support messages are retained for limited operational periods, and backups can take additional time to cycle out.

## Security

We use reasonable safeguards such as encrypted connections, access controls, protected authentication, updates, and rate limiting. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## International processing

The site and its providers may process information in more than one country. Where required, recognized safeguards should be used for international transfers.

## Your privacy rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access personal information associated with you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion.
- Restrict or object to certain processing.
- Withdraw consent.
- Receive portable information where applicable.
- Appeal or complain to a privacy authority.
- Opt out of certain targeted advertising, sale, or sharing definitions under regional law.

To make a request, email [support@73-9.org](mailto:support@73-9.org) from the address connected to the account when possible. Describe the request and the relevant account or result. We may need to verify identity before acting.

You can also clear browser storage without contacting us.

## Children’s privacy

73-9 is intended for a general audience and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 or the minimum age required in their region without appropriate authorization.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it where required.

## U.S. state privacy choices

Some U.S. state laws define targeted advertising or “sharing” broadly. Where required, applicable opt-out or restricted-data-processing controls should be made available through the advertising provider or a site privacy control.

We may update this Privacy Policy when features, providers, laws, or practices change. The “Last updated” date shows the current version. Material changes may be announced through a notice on the site.
