## Get in touch

We read every message about the free 73-9 browser challenge. The best way to reach us is email.

## Support email

**[support@73-9.org](mailto:support@73-9.org)**

Use this address for:

- Bug reports and gameplay issues
- Feedback and feature ideas
- Privacy, cookies, or terms questions
- Partnership or press inquiries

## What to include

For bugs, please include:

- The page or step where it happened (for example during a wheel spin or simulation)
- Browser and device (Chrome on macOS, Safari on iPhone, etc.)
- A short description of what you expected vs. what you saw

## Response time

We aim to reply within a few business days. Complex technical issues may take longer. The game itself stays free to play while you wait—no account required.
