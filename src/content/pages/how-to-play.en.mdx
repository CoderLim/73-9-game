## The goal

**73-9** is a free browser challenge at [73-9.org](https://73-9.org). You get one hundred million dollars and five spins of a wheel. Build a starting five that can stand up to the 2015-16 Golden State Warriors—the club that finished 73-9.

## Step-by-step

1. **Spin the wheel** — It lands on a random team-season anywhere from 1990 to today. That roster is your market for this pick.
2. **Sign exactly one player** — Choose one name for the current position and pay what that deal would cost under the 2026 salary cap.
3. **Stay under $100M** — Repeat through point guard, shooting guard, small forward, power forward, and center. Overspend early and the last seats become minimum-salary scraps.
4. **Simulate the season** — When the lineup locks, run an 82-game simulation and see how often your five topples the Warriors who authored 73-9.

Optional re-spins tax the same bankroll, so every reload is a strategic choice—not a free mulligan.

## What you are deciding each spin

You are not drafting from the entire league. Each stop of the wheel narrows the market to one franchise season. The tension is always the same: take the megastar and starve the other four spots, or hunt value and stitch five complementary pieces together before the money runs out.

## After the sim

The result card shows your projected win percentage against the 2015-16 Warriors, plus the strongest legal five you could have signed from the same five team-seasons under the same cap. Read that gap—then open a new run and try again.

Signed-in players can save results and appear on the [leaderboard](/leaderboard). Guests can still play the full game for free.

## Quick tips

- Do not max out on the first two picks unless you have a plan for the other three.
- A quietly elite season can beat a household name that empties the bankroll.
- Compare your lineup to the “best squad you could have signed” after every sim—that is the fastest way to improve.
- Every run reshuffles the wheel, so every bankroll puzzle is new.
