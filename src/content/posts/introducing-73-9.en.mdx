**73-9.org** is a single-purpose home for one basketball puzzle: build a five-man lineup that can stand up to the **2015–16 Golden State Warriors** — the team that finished **73–9**, the best regular-season record in NBA history.

This site is not a game aggregator. There is no menu of unrelated titles. You land on the court, draft, simulate, and climb the board.

## The challenge

You start with a **$100 million** budget. Five times, a reel lands on a random **team-season** (roughly 1990 through today). From that roster you may **sign exactly one player**. Cap hits are scaled to today’s money, so a max deal from an old season still has to fit your remaining budget.

When the five slots are filled, you run a season simulation against the 73–9 Warriors wall. Win percentage, share cards, and — if you are signed in — a saved run on the public **leaderboard**.

Need a do-over on a bad land? **Re-roll** costs **$10M** from the same budget. Spend wisely.

## How a run flows

1. **Start drafting** from the home court card.
2. **Spin** for a franchise season, then pick one affordable player for the open slot.
3. Fill all five positions (you can swap slot order before you sim).
4. **Sim the season**, share the card, or draft again.
5. Sign in if you want that run counted on the leaderboard.

The full rules also live on the homepage **About** section and the [About](/about) page — the game itself stays focused on the draft.

## What you will find on the platform

- **Play** (`/`) — the live draft board over the arena backdrop
- **Leaderboard** (`/leaderboard`) — best saved runs from signed-in players
- **Blog** (`/blog`) — notes like this one: how the game works and site updates
- **About / legal** — background on 73–9, contact, and policies

Guests can play freely. Unsaved guest runs are fine for casual spins; create an account when you want a permanent place on the board.

## Why “73-9”

Fans already use **73-9** as shorthand for that Warriors season. The game turns the number into a playable bar: not “guess trivia,” but **allocate a salary cap under randomness** and see whether your five can hang with the best regular-season team ever.

Open [73-9.org](/), hit **Start drafting**, and take your shot at the wall.
