## 1. 73-9.org: best salary-cap lineup builder

73-9 is one of the **NBA lineup builder games** where price matters as much as reputation. You receive $100 million, five open positions, and a random team-season in each round. You sign one player-season from each market.

The completed five is simulated across 82 games and compared with the 2015-16 Warriors. A second lineup shows a stronger legal five that could have been selected from the same markets.

### Why choose it

Among **NBA lineup builder games**, 73-9 is strongest when you want every contract to affect later picks. An elite first player can improve the ceiling while making the final four positions harder to solve.

The comparison roster also separates wheel luck from decision quality, a feature not all **NBA lineup builder games** provide. Both lineups had the same opportunities.

### Best for

- Salary-cap strategy.
- Historical player-seasons.
- A clear opponent and result.
- Reviewing missed value after the draft.

### Limitation

73-9 is not a current-prospect mock draft. Like other simulated **NBA lineup builder games**, it is entertainment rather than a real-world prediction.

## 2. 82-0: best quick all-time challenge

82-0 became one of the most visible **NBA lineup builder games** in 2026. Each position receives a random team and era. You choose an eligible player, complete the five, and receive a simulated record.

Limited reshuffles create the central decision: accept a very good option now or gamble for a legend later.

### Why choose it

82-0 is fast, easy to understand, and difficult to master. Among quick **NBA lineup builder games**, it creates tension without requiring contract research. Its HoopIQ mode hides statistics and rewards deeper basketball memory.

A June 2026 review from [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/944975/82-0-perfect-basketball-stats-game) highlighted the mix of fantasy-style roster thinking, randomness, and quick feedback.

### Best for

- Short all-time drafts.
- Team-and-era knowledge.
- Sharing one lineup and score.
- Players who prefer lighter rules than cap-heavy **NBA lineup builder games**.

### Limitation

82-0 focuses less on contracts and front-office management than some other **NBA lineup builder games**.

## 3. NBA Grids Draft Day: best daily competition

[Draft Day](https://www.nbagrids.com/) is one of the **NBA lineup builder games** built around a shared daily slate. Everyone receives the same five spins. Each round draws a team and decade, and the user chooses a position-eligible player.

A completed lineup can face a Hall of Fame squad, a friend, or another player.

### Why choose it

Shared conditions make results easier to compare. This is one reason daily **NBA lineup builder games** can produce more meaningful leaderboards. When users receive the same draws, the result reflects choices rather than completely different luck.

### Best for

- Daily play.
- Friend challenges.
- Position-based drafting.
- Competitive **NBA lineup builder games** with common conditions.

### Limitation

A daily format may be less useful when you want unlimited independent runs.

## 4. HoopsMatic Draft Simulator: best 2026 mock draft

[HoopsMatic](https://hoopsmatic.com/draft-simulator) sits beside historical **NBA lineup builder games**, but its main draft tool serves prospect intent.

The 2026 simulator lets you control one NBA team, simulate the entire draft, auto-fill picks, or manage all 30 teams manually. It produces a draft recap rather than an all-time five.

### Why choose it

HoopsMatic connects its draft simulator with a trade machine and roster manager, making it broader than many standalone **NBA lineup builder games**. Users can move from prospect questions to contracts and transactions within the same tool family.

### Best for

- The 2026 NBA Draft.
- One-team mock drafts.
- Full 60-pick boards.
- Shareable prospect scenarios.

### Limitation

A prospect simulator is not a replacement for historical **NBA lineup builder games**. It answers who a team should draft, not which legends form the best five.

## 5. NBA Expansion Draft Simulator: best franchise build

[NBA Expansion Draft Simulator](https://www.nbaexpansion.com/) asks users to construct a Seattle or Las Vegas roster. Its expansion mode uses exposed players, while free-build mode opens a broader pool.

Unlike five-player **NBA lineup builder games**, the expansion format requires depth, contracts, and a franchise plan.

### Why choose it

The tool makes users consider more than a starting unit. Few **NBA lineup builder games** ask for both a roster core and bench depth. You need positional coverage, acceptable salaries, and players who can become the foundation of a new team.

### Best for

- Current-player roster construction.
- Seattle or Las Vegas scenarios.
- Expansion rules.
- Users who want more depth than standard **NBA lineup builder games**.

### Limitation

No official expansion process has finalized these exact rules. Treat the tool as a scenario, not an official league simulator.

## 6. Build a Hooper: best attribute builder

[Build a Hooper](https://www.buildhooper.com/) is adjacent to traditional **NBA lineup builder games** because it builds one fictional player rather than a five.

The user steals attributes from players on random team-season rosters. Classic mode shows ratings, Blind hides ratings, and Chaos also hides position information.

### Why choose it

Attribute drafting creates arguments that whole-player **NBA lineup builder games** cannot. It also shows how **NBA lineup builder games** can focus on skills rather than complete players. You can combine shooting, defense, athleticism, and other skills from different sources.

### Best for

- Quick replayable builds.
- Player-attribute debates.
- Blind knowledge modes.
- Sharing a composite player.

### Limitation

Users searching specifically for five-player **NBA lineup builder games** should begin with 73-9, 82-0, or Draft Day.

## How to choose NBA lineup builder games

The best **NBA lineup builder games** match the type of roster question you want to answer. Start by deciding what you want **NBA lineup builder games** to simulate.

### Historical players or prospects?

Choose historical **NBA lineup builder games** when you want established players, seasons, teams, or eras. Choose HoopsMatic when the current prospect board and real pick order matter.

### Starting five or full roster?

73-9, 82-0, and Draft Day build a five. Expansion tools build a deeper roster. Build a Hooper creates one composite player.

### Salary cap or random draw?

Cap-based **NBA lineup builder games** emphasize value. Team-and-era tools emphasize knowledge and reroll timing. Mock drafts emphasize pick order and team need.

### Solo replay or shared competition?

73-9 and 82-0 work well for repeated solo attempts. Draft Day is stronger when you want common daily conditions.
