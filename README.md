## What is 73-9?

In 2015–16 the Golden State Warriors went **73–9**, the best regular-season record in NBA history. This free browser game turns that benchmark into a puzzle:

You get **$100 million**, **five spins** of a wheel, and one job — build a starting five that can stand up to that team.

## How to play

1. **Spin the wheel** — it lands on a random team-season from 1990 to today.
2. **Sign one player** from that roster — only one, no cherry-picking the whole league.
3. **Repeat five times** — one signing per position — while staying under the $100M budget.
4. **Simulate** an 82-game season and see how often your five beats the Warriors.

Every contract is priced against the **2026 salary cap**. Stars eat the bankroll; value finds win the night. Optional re-spins cost cash from the same pile, so every reload is a choice.

When the sim ends, you also see the **best legal five** you could have signed from those same five team-seasons — same luck, perfect decisions. Share your result and challenge friends.
